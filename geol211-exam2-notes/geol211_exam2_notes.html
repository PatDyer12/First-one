Why care? wind starts ocean circulation · ocean + air trade

1. Air Density — how + why it changes
Air moves 2 ways:
  HORIZONTAL (along surface) → PRESSURE: flows HIGH → LOW
KEY
  VERTICAL → DENSITY: less dense air RISES, dense air sinks
  + Earth's rotation (Coriolis) changes the direction
Sun = ultimate energy source for ocean + wind circulation
Air density is controlled by 2 things:
★
  1) TEMPERATURE: warm air → molecules spread out → fewer
     per volume → LESS dense → RISES
     cold air → molecules packed close → MORE dense → SINKS
  2) MOISTURE: water vapor H2O (18) is LIGHTER than
     N2 (28, 78% of air) + O2 (32, 21% of air)
     → humid air is LESS dense than dry air → rises
★ trick
∴ Densest air = POLES (cold + dry)
  least dense = equator (warm + humid)

3. Global Atmospheric Circulation ★ (DRAW IT)

9. Predicting a Hurricane's Path ★
Hurricanes are STEERED by winds around H + L systems
Wind flows H → L · storm can't push INTO a high → goes
  AROUND its edge (CLOCKWISE around a H in NH)
  + gets pulled toward LOWS / troughs
Typical Atlantic track: ① forms off Africa, trades push W
  ② reaches W edge of Bermuda High → turns N
  ③ meets westerlies (~30°N) → curves NE, out to sea
Strong High stretching W → storm keeps going W → FL/Gulf
Weak High / High shifted E → turns N early → out to sea
  or up the East Coast · LOW over E US pulls storm N

16. Upwelling + Downwelling ★
UPWELLING = deep, cold, NUTRIENT-RICH water rises
DEF
DOWNWELLING = surface water piles up + SINKS
DEF
Coastal, NH west coast (e.g. California):
  wind from NORTH → Ekman 90° right → water moves OFFSHORE
  → deep water rises to replace it = UPWELLING
  wind from SOUTH → water moves ONSHORE → piles up at
  coast → sinks = DOWNWELLING
EQUATORIAL: Coriolis turns water RIGHT north of equator +
  LEFT south of it → water DIVERGES → UPWELLING
SOUTHERN OCEAN: winds around Antarctica → upwelling
GYRE CENTER: water CONVERGES → DOWNWELLING
MARINE LIFE:
★

  surface stays nutrient-poor → low productivity

18. Deep Water Circulation + Climate ★
Deep water FORMS at poles: cooling + sea ice (salty) →
  dense → SINKS → spreads along bottom → slowly rises
  AABW = Antarctic Bottom Water (densest, Weddell Sea)
  NADW = North Atlantic Deep Water (near Greenland)
  Pacific deep water: weak layers, sluggish, uniform
CONVEYOR BELT: warm shallow current → N Atlantic → gives
  heat to air → cold + salty → SINKS → deep current S →
  Antarctica → Indian + Pacific → rises → back (~1000 yrs)
CLIMATE EFFECTS:
★
  moves heat equator → poles → keeps N Europe mild
  carries O2 + CO2 (dissolved gases) into the deep ocean
IF IT SLOWS: melting ice + rain → FRESHER, less dense water
  → can't sink → conveyor slows → less heat → N. cools
Happened before: YOUNGER DRYAS (~12,900–11,500 yrs ago)
  Lake Agassiz meltwater → NH back to near-glacial cold
  · ended fast: Greenland +10°C in about a decade
Today: AMOC slowed in 20th century → "cold blob" S of
  Greenland cooled while the rest of Earth warmed
