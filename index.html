      How the model works

      Two independent reads on every player, blended, then run through your actual lineup.

      A

      Projection model

      Sleeper's week-by-week rest-of-season projections, scored for your league. Each week a player projects above replacement level counts; below it counts zero. Byes and injuries show up as zero weeks automatically.

      B

      Trade market

      FantasyCalc values come from thousands of real trades in real leagues, matched to your format (redraft or dynasty, 1QB or superflex). It's what managers actually pay for a player.

      C

      Blend

      The model is rescaled onto the market's units, then mixed. Default mix is 50/50 in redraft and 30/70 toward the market in dynasty, where future years matter more than this season's projections. Drag the slider to change it.

      D

      Consolidation

      Two quarters ≠ a fifty-cent piece. Each side's best asset counts 100%, then 88%, 76%, 64%… (floor 40%). The side getting the best player gets rewarded for it.

      E

      Lineup impact

      Your best legal lineup is rebuilt before and after the trade (flex and superflex included). The difference is projected starter points per week for the rest of the season. It's the "does this actually help me win" check.

      F

      Signals

      When the model rates a player ≥30% above what the market charges, it's a Buy low. When it's ≥30% below, Sell high. That's the market mispricing gap, same idea as a stock trading away from fair value.
