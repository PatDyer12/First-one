Sample data The board came with example bets so you could see it working. Clear them before you log your own.
Win sound
Tap Play to hear one, then pick it. Your pick plays on every device you're signed in on. Bets of 1.5u or more get the whale, 3u or more get the nuke. Odds of +150 or longer get the sniper, with bigger versions at +200, +300 and +400 (a whale or nuke sized long shot gets both).
Reaction clips Only the good stuff, and only for your side. Your team's touchdowns, field goals, turnovers, red-zone trips, home runs and goals get a clip, with your team's own clips first. While you're winning, your winning clips play every 2½ minutes. When it's close, a filler or some hype plays every 3 minutes. When you're down but within two scores, comeback clips play. When you're getting blown out, it stays quiet. Overs and unders are judged on pace, and stay quiet whenever they're off pace. Tap the screen to close a clip. Turn this off on your phone and leave it on for the monitor. ESPN's scores run ahead of your TV. The board holds scores, clips and win/loss animations back by this much so nothing spoils a play. If clips still beat the TV, turn it up. If they come late, turn it down. Each screen keeps its own setting.
Ticker Runs along the bottom on game days: every FBS score (yours first), touchdowns and field goals as they happen, upset watch when a ranked team is in trouble, crunch time, and injury news.
