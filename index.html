Sample data The board came with example bets so you could see it working. Clear them before you log your own.
Win sound
Tap Play to hear one, then pick it. Your pick plays on every device you're signed in on. Bets of 1.5u or more get the whale, 3u or more get the nuke. Odds of +150 or longer get the sniper, with bigger versions at +200, +300 and +400 (a whale or nuke sized long shot gets both).
Goldie intros Every win gets one, and a loss gets Goldie wiping out (or one of your loss clips). Regular hits mostly get the Goldie scenes made from his pictures (skydive, whip, wheelie, war, strapped, smoke, aura, make it rain, takeover), mixed in with the cartoon ones. $100+ wins get the blockbuster, parlays get their own, and +150 or longer gets the sniper. Tap any to watch it.
Reaction clips Only the good stuff, and only for your side. Your team's touchdowns, field goals, turnovers, red-zone trips, home runs and goals get a clip, with your team's own clips first. While you're winning, your winning clips play every 2½ minutes. When it's close, a music break or some hype plays every 3 minutes. Music breaks are songs only, with a card showing the song and artist. With only 1 or 2 bets out, there's more dead air, so a clip comes every 2 minutes. When you're down but within two scores, comeback clips play. When you're getting blown out, it stays quiet. Overs and unders are judged on pace, and stay quiet whenever they're off pace. Tap the screen to close a clip. Turn this off on your phone and leave it on for the monitor.
The trial replays the latest finished NFL game play by play with a pretend bet on the winner: play alerts and clips when your side scores, quiet when they don't, then the win. Nothing gets saved and it doesn't touch your numbers. If the TV cuts off the edges of the board (the logo, Exit TV, the ticker), slide this up in TV mode until all four corners show, or press − and + on the keyboard. Best fix is the TV's own Screen Fit picture size or Underscan in the Mac's Display settings; this is the backup. Each screen keeps its own setting. ESPN's scores run ahead of your TV. The board holds scores, clips and win/loss animations back by this much so nothing spoils a play. If clips still beat the TV, turn it up. If they come late, turn it down. Each screen keeps its own setting.
Main sport
What the ticker follows (every score, spreads and totals before kickoff, upset watch, injury news) and which league the bet form opens to. Auto runs college Tuesday through Saturday and the NFL on Sunday, Monday and Thursday. Applies to every screen.

Slate mode
Auto goes by how many games are on. 1 PM mode (5+ games): touchdown and turnover alerts on your games, and every touchdown for your side gets a music edit after its alert (a Goldie edit, a hype song or DJ Goldie); in between it's DJ Goldie and music every couple of minutes, no other clips. 4 PM mode (2 to 4 games) and prime time (one game): the full show, plus something every couple of minutes (every minute in prime time, mostly his scenes), taking turns: DJ Goldie (off your Spotify playlist when it's connected), a football edit and one of his scenes. Each screen keeps its own setting.
