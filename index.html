        How the model works

        Public market first. Every factor weighted by how well it actually predicted results in 2022–2025. One fixed formula.

        1

        Start from the public market

        Every player starts at his FantasyCalc value from before the season (value minus its 30-day change), matched to your format. That's the market's read before any games were played. Healthy players who haven't played yet use today's market.

        2

        Learn from 2022–2025

        For every player-season in the last four years, we took everything known after Week 2 and measured how well each factor predicted his points per game the rest of the year, and the next year. A ridge regression gives each factor a weight. Factors that predicted well get big weights, and weak ones shrink toward zero.

        3

        Prove it on unseen seasons

        Each model is tested on a season it didn't train on. For each position it keeps the factor set that tested best: all factors, a core set, or preseason info alone. The scores are below.

        4

        Turn points into value

        The model's projection ÷ what preseason info alone predicts = how much a player's outlook changed. The market's own price curve, fit from FantasyCalc, turns that into value: value moves about 3× as fast as points per game in redraft and about 1.6× in dynasty.

        5

        Fixed mix by league

        Redraft uses the rest-of-season model. Keeper uses 50% this season and 50% next season. Dynasty uses 35% and 65%. Roster fit is a fixed 25% / 20% / 10% of your lineup gain, capped at ±15% of the deal so it only tips close calls. Nothing is adjustable, so the formula stays the same.

        6

        Injuries & suspensions

        Expected weeks out is the longest of: weeks projected at 0 points from now, the status minimum (Out/Doubtful/Suspended 1 week, IR/PUP 4), and season-ending injuries (ACL, Achilles, Lisfranc). Value × (share of the season available)1.5. Out all season in redraft = 0. In keeper/dynasty only this season's share (50% / 35%) is lost.

        7

        Signals

        Buy low means the model value is ≥15% above today's market. Sell high means it's ≥15% below. That gap is where you win trades.

        What the model learned: factor weights

        Share of each position's rest-of-season model. R² = share of the variation in future points explained, on seasons the model never saw.
