      Show your work

      1. Adjust for scoring. Projections are stored as PPR points per game. Half-PPR subtracts 0.5 × receptions/gm; Standard subtracts the full receptions.
      2. Find replacement level. A player is only worth what he scores above someone you could grab off waivers. Replacement is the roughly-last-starter at each position, so it rises in smaller leagues (deeper waivers) and QB replacement drops hard in Superflex (everyone starts two).
      3. VORP per week = adjusted PPG − replacement PPG, floored at 0. A below-replacement guy is worth about nothing: you'd cut him.
      4. Multiply by weeks he'll actually play. Weeks left minus weeks missed for injury. In Dynasty, add a future chunk: 17 wks × age multiplier, since a 23-year-old RB has years left and a 30-year-old doesn't.
      5. Stud premium. Two quarters don't equal a fifty-cent piece in fantasy, because you can only start so many players. Each side's best player counts 100%, the second 90%, third 80%, and so on (min 50%). The extra roster spot you give up is already covered: the guy you'd cut is replacement-level, worth 0.
      6. Compare. The gap as a % of the bigger side decides the call: under 8% is fair, 8–20% leans one way, more than 20% is lopsided.
